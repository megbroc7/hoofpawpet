## A simple review routine

Owner: Sheryl. Use the same request for all eligible clients, regardless of how positive their feedback is. No gifts, discounts, five-star wording, or prewritten customer reviews.

- After the first completed booking, send one personal request with the final visit update or within 24–48 hours. For a regular client, ask after the first week of completed visits.
- If there is no review after seven days, send one gentle reminder, then stop. Do not repeatedly ask established clients who have already reviewed.
- Each Friday, spend ten minutes checking the profile: reply to new reviews, record requests, and check for any unresolved customer concern.
- Aim to reply within two working days. Use first-person singular, acknowledge only details the customer made public, and avoid details about their address, travel plans, access arrangements or pet's health.
- Track only: client name, completed booking date, first request date, optional reminder date, review received, reply posted. Keep this in Sheryl's existing private client records.
